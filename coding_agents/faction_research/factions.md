# The 24 faction options, ranked for implementation

Research snapshot: September 19, 2026. [Source authority and archive](README.md). Difficulty is an engineering assessment of rules, persistence, interface, and fair AI support in this repository—not faction strength. Ordering within a band is approximate. Existing factions are shown first; they are not new work equivalent to an expansion faction.

The collection counts **24 selectable options**, including two Lyra versions. Its three additional Terran front sheets are not three additional mechanical species. Printed setup, resource tracks, ship costs, permanent stats, and slot diagrams must be transcribed as fixtures before any addition ships; the summaries below are not replacements for those sheets.

## Source discrepancies and implementation decisions still needed

- **Daedalus setup:** the combined Drive info sheet and separate board both show three colony ships (OCR initially misread four). However, the combined sheet labels the home sector 278 while the board labels it 274. Use the actual archived starting-sector tile and resolve this mismatch when transcribing setup; do not infer identifiers from OCR or filenames.
- **Older printed trade rates:** use the May 2026 explicit list, not a stale 3:1 label. `New Trade Rates.jpg` contains replacement graphics but does not label every faction, so it is not sufficient alone to assign rates.
- **Dorado examples:** the old multi-tile round-two example conflicts with the explicit 2026 placement limit. The explicit amendment takes precedence; examples need adaptation.
- **Et’Etn’K’Tis outer-sector exception:** its faction document names Planta and Et’Etn’K’Tis, while the May 2026 house rules also name Midas. Use the newer explicit global rule for the complete variant.
- **Research scope:** the Conspiracy’s bay/tractor rules, every Evolution/Distortion tile, all alternate track cells, and exact setup diagrams require a full implementation transcription and fixture audit. The research pass verifies the defining mechanics and dependency ranking; it does not certify every component value for immediate coding.
- **Ruleset packaging:** source authority does not mean every file describes one simultaneously enabled mode. The Drive also contains an optional slower/less-aggressive variant that restricts ancient parts and explicitly discourages Magellan/Mega War. Keep selectable modes explicit.

## Suggested delivery order

1. **Shared prerequisites:** optional content-pack/ruleset selection pinned on the match; faction identity independent of persisted seat color; directional/batch trade; verified new home sectors. Preserve current saves and room validation.
2. **First playable group:** Rho Indi, Magellan, Midas, Ragnarok. This exercises new scoring/economy hooks and mixed actions without a new resource or entirely new ship lifecycle.
3. **Tracks and structures:** Infinimperium, Dread Armada, Exiles, both Lyra versions, then Daedalus and Conspiracy. Add scenario fixtures and AI support with each mechanic.
4. **Major systems separately:** Mega War, Et’Etn’K’Tis, Octantis, Swarm, Dorado, Pyxis. These deserve their own plans and visual prototypes, not a bulk catalog import.

Every new mechanism must use identical legality for human and AI seats, survive reconnection mid-choice, expose understandable costs and effects, and remain bounded in AI candidate generation. No new faction is enabled by this research or the registry refactor.
