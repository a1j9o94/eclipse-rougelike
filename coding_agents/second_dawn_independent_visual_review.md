# Independent actual-game interface review

Outcome: a player can understand the board and prepare legal tactical choices at laptop and desktop sizes. This is a separate agent review of the actual `SecondDawnBoard`, not the earlier prototype and not a human playtest.

## Initial rendered-image review

Reviewed 1366×768 opening, late, blueprint editor and combat, plus 1920×1080 late, from `second_dawn_gameplay_screenshots`. The visual treatment has coherent navy panels, restrained gold emphasis, readable resource totals and distinct numbered ownership marks. Found material defects independently also identified by the rules reviewer:

- At laptop size the sixth civilization is outside the visible roster, including when it is the human seat.
- Galaxy fit leaves excessive empty space while reducing sector/fleet labels to illegible sizes, especially in a crowded position.
- Blueprint editing places derived statistics and confirmation below the visible region; oversized row spacing contributes.
- Combat allocation has no immediately visible sector, opposing fleet or target durability context.
- The Explore inspector remains unrelated to active blueprint/combat workflows.
- Some editor part names display raw IDs rather than human-readable names.

Sent these to the visual owner, who is revising and regenerating screenshots. Initial screenshots are not approved baselines.
