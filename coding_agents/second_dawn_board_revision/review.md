### Source correction: aggression timing

Re-read publisher-verified [Dized diplomacy rules](https://rules.dized.com/game/dS7ANw3JR-O-HIg-7k5qVA/R-z7j2POR3iJtjDRFg3Ahg/diplomacy-4-players), specifically the aggression definition and unpinned transit exception. The rule checks ship presence at the end of the player's **Action**, not on entering a sector or after each Move activation. The existing end-action engine check is therefore correct. UI wording was corrected from “entering” to “ending your action”; transit through a partner's sector is explicitly explained. A new public command-processor regression enters and leaves the partner's sector in two Move activations, then ends the action and preserves diplomacy. Ending instead after the first activation breaks the relation and presents the persisted population-return choice. Both timing and traitor-transfer tests pass; no engine changes were made.
