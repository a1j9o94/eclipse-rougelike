# Visual sector Build planner

Outcome: choose a controlled sector, adjust visual ship/structure quantities and confirm one complete order with any required conversion and upkeep preview.

BuildPlanner owns an accessible native modal and selected-sector dropdown. Six component cards reuse ship silhouettes and the established resource/structure icons. Each has count steppers, printed faction material price, remaining ship supply or structure effect, and technology/supply explanations. Existing Build activations (including reaction capacity and Nanorobots) constrain the total. The order uses one ordinary build command or atomic trade-and-act; conversion selection and ActionEconomy are reused from the purchase interface. The footer stays outside the scrolling body. Funded orders explicitly say “Convert & build”; ordinary orders say “Confirm build”. Escape/Close/Cancel discard the editable order.

Legality matches the authoritative engine: active surviving seat, no pending decision, action phase, controlled sector, compatible open action or available influence disc, remaining activations, required technologies, global per-color ship supply, one structure of each kind in a sector, and affordable direct or funded total. Building in your controlled sector with enemy ships is not prohibited by the existing rules implementation; the UI does not invent that restriction. Global orbital/monolith physical counts are not capped by the current engine and no UI-only cap was introduced.

Four focused component tests cover a real legal multi-ship order, activation limits, technology and exhausted supply reasons, disconnected submission, conversion plus purchase accepted by the actual engine, removal of draft quantities, and eliminated-seat blocking. Missing component and eliminated-seat/explicit conversion-label expectations failed before their implementations. Tests now pass. Updated action-workflows executes Build through this modal and Move through ship checkbox/map destination/confirmation; all11 actual browser workflows pass.

Independent visual review assigned to effects_revision inspected funded orders at1366×768,1440×900,1920×1080. All six cards, conversion, upkeep and confirmation fit. It found global reset overrode dialog centering; effects_revision added explicit margin:auto and is recapturing the final centered modal. Evidence is reported separately from human playtesting.
